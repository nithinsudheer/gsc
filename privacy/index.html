Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GetSetCollab, operated by GetSet Ventures ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use getsetcollab.com and our services (the "Platform"). By using the Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information directly from you:

  • Name / Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing address
  • Age
  • Password (stored in encrypted form)

We also collect information automatically through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5), as well as any information you provide when you contact us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: create and manage your account; provide and operate the Platform, including facilitating collaborations and payments; process transactions through our payment partners; communicate with you, including sending service messages and — where you opt in — newsletters and marketing emails; improve and secure the Platform; and comply with our legal obligations.

4. Marketing Communications

If you opt in, we may send you newsletters and promotional emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting hello@getsetcollab.com.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, local storage, and session storage to operate the Platform, remember your preferences, and understand usage. We also use:

  • Analytics tools (for example, Google Analytics) to measure traffic and improve the Platform.
  • Google Maps APIs to provide location-related features.
  • Google Login (social login) to let you sign in with your Google account.

You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect Platform functionality.

6. Payments

Payments are processed by third-party payment gateways. We do not store your full debit or credit card numbers on our servers; card data is handled by our PCI-compliant payment partners under their own privacy policies.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with: service providers who help us operate the Platform (for example, hosting, analytics, and payment processing); other users only as necessary to facilitate a collaboration you participate in; and authorities where required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to or intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption of passwords. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw your consent, by contacting hello@getsetcollab.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

11. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Platform and comply with our legal obligations.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@getsetcollab.com, or write to GetSet Ventures, Velekkatt House, Edamuttam, Thrissur, Kerala, 680568, India.
